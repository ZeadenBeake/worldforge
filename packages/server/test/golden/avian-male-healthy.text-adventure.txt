Kestrel
Avian humanoid, 31 years old (adult).
Height: 178 cm
Weight: 66 kg

Features:
- a body that generates strong electrical current, usable as a weapon or a power source
- a humanoid frame
- a showy crest of head feathers
- can emit an electromagnetic pulse strong enough to disable electronics
- capable of flight
- lays eggs rather than bearing live young
- feathers covering the whole body
- a hooked beak in place of a mouth
- slate-grey plumage
- barred feathers
- reproduces sexually
- a pair of feathered wings

He tends to:
- preens constantly
- holds that perching above others is shameful

Relationships:
- He is friend of Wren (old friends)
